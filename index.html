About VRWS

Voice of Rohingya Women and Students (VRWS) is a dedicated community-based organization led by Rohingya women. We are passionately committed to empowering Rohingya women, girls, youths, students, and marginalized groups, while fiercely advocating for their fundamental human rights and fostering sustainable community peacebuilding.


Our Vision & Mission

To establish a dignified, peaceful, and inclusive society where every individual enjoys equal human rights, safety, and opportunities for growth.

Core Activities

  • Women & Youth Empowerment: Creating dynamic platforms for leadership development, skill building, and self-reliance.
  • Education & Mentorship: Supporting educational initiatives, learning circles, and academic support for children and youth.
  • Human Rights Advocacy: Raising strong voices against injustice, advocating for dignity, safety, and social justice.
  • Peacebuilding & Cohesion: Fostering social harmony, dialogue, and resilience within the community.
