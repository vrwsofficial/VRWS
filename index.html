About Us

Voice of Rohingya Women and Students (VRWS) is a community-based organization led by Rohingya women, empowering Rohingya women, girls, youths, students and LGBTQ individuals, while advocating for the rights of the Rohingya community and promoting peacebuilding.

Our Vision

To build a dignified, peaceful, and inclusive society where every individual enjoys fundamental human rights, equality, and safety.

Our Mission

To uplift vulnerable groups through education, advocacy, leadership training, and active community participation.

Our Core Activities

  • Women & Youth Empowerment: Creating platforms for leadership and skill development.
  • Education & Learning: Supporting educational initiatives for children and youth.
  • Human Rights Advocacy: Raising awareness on rights, dignity, and social justice.
  • Peacebuilding: Fostering social cohesion and community harmony.
