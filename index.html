About VRWS

Voice of Rohingya Women and Students (VRWS) is a community-based organization led by Rohingya women, dedicated to empowering Rohingya women, girls, youths, students, and LGBTQ individuals, while vigorously advocating for the fundamental rights of the Rohingya community and promoting sustainable peacebuilding.


Our Vision & Mission

To establish a dignified, peaceful, and inclusive society where every individual enjoys fundamental human rights, equality, and safety.

Core Activities

  • Women & Youth Empowerment: Creating platforms for leadership and professional skill development.
  • Education & Learning: Supporting educational initiatives and mentorship for children and youth.
  • Human Rights Advocacy: Raising awareness on rights, dignity, and social justice.
  • Peacebuilding: Fostering social cohesion, dialogue, and community harmony.
