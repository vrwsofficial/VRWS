About VRWS

Voice of Rohingya Women and Students (VRWS) is a community-based organization led by Rohingya women, empowering Rohingya women, girls, youths, students and LGBTQ individuals, while advocating for the rights of the Rohingya community and promoting peacebuilding.


Our Vision & Mission

To establish a peaceful, inclusive, and rights-based society through education, leadership development, and active community participation.

Core Activities

  • Women & Youth Empowerment through skill programs.
  • Educational initiatives and mentorship for students.
  • Human rights advocacy and community protection.
  • Peacebuilding and social harmony programs.
